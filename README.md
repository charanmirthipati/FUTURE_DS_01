# FUTURE_DS_01
Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship (Task 1).

The objective was to analyze business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, and regional performance, and to provide actionable business recommendations.

Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Dataset

Online Retail Dataset

Analysis Performed
Revenue Calculation

Revenue was calculated using:

Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price

Top Revenue-Generating Products

The analysis identified the highest-performing products based on total revenue.

Top Product:

DOTCOM POSTAGE (£206,245)
Revenue by Country

Top performing countries:

United Kingdom (£8.18 Million)
Netherlands (£284,662)
EIRE (£263,277)
Germany (£221,698)
France (£197,404)
Revenue Trend Analysis

Revenue comparison by year:

2010: £748,957
2011: £8,998,791
Key Insights
Revenue increased significantly from 2010 to 2011.
United Kingdom generated the highest sales revenue.
A small group of products contributed a large percentage of overall revenue.
European countries represented the strongest international markets.
Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts on top-performing products.
Strengthen business operations in high-performing countries.
Improve inventory planning using historical sales trends.
Expand customer acquisition efforts in key international markets.
